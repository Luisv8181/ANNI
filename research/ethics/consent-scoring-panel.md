# Consent & Recruitment — Blind Scoring Panel (template)

Draft template for consenting the independent scorers. Heath/IRB to finalize wording; don't use until the
IRB approves. A parallel version will be needed for the later real-therapist phase.

## Recruitment message (template)
> Subject: Invitation — brief paid/volunteer rating task on AI mental-health responses
>
> Hello [name], we're a student research team (Sanofi Biomedical Science Program, Geisinger) studying how
> conversational AI handles anxiety and crisis signals. We're inviting licensed mental-health
> professionals to **blind-score** short response excerpts on safety, accuracy, and warmth. It takes about
> [N] minutes per set, is done on your own time, and you can stop anytime. Some excerpts contain simulated
> crisis content. Interested? Reply and we'll send details and a consent form.

## Informed consent (template — key elements)
- **Purpose:** rate short, de-identified response excerpts to help evaluate AI safety in mental-health
  conversations.
- **What you'll do:** score [N] items on safety/accuracy/warmth, note the risk you perceive, and guess
  whether each came from a human or AI. ~[N] minutes.
- **Voluntary & withdrawal:** participation is voluntary; you may skip items or stop at any time with no
  penalty; you may withdraw your data before [analysis date].
- **Risks:** some excerpts depict **simulated** crisis/distress (feeling-level only; no methods). If this
  is distressing, skip and/or stop. This is not a clinical duty and no real person is awaiting your reply.
- **No real patients:** all patient content is **synthetic**; you are not treating anyone.
- **Confidentiality:** your identity is stored as a **code**, separate from your ratings; results are
  reported in aggregate.
- **Compensation:** [state amount, or "volunteer"].
- **Contact:** [PI / team contact] and [IRB contact].
- **Consent:** "I have read the above, my questions were answered, and I agree to participate." ☐ Agree

## Scorer-facing safety note (include in the packet)
> These excerpts are from a research simulation. If reviewing this content is distressing, pause or stop.
> If you or someone you know is in crisis, contact 988 (US) or local emergency services.

Starter drafts for the ethics side of the study. **Heath owns this** (IRB, licensing); these files are
scaffolds to fill in and take to the IRB — not final, not legal advice. Nothing here authorizes data
collection on its own.

## Files
- [`IRB-protocol-outline.md`](IRB-protocol-outline.md) — the sections an IRB submission needs, pre-filled
  from our study so far.
- [`data-management-plan.md`](data-management-plan.md) — what data we hold, where it lives, retention,
  de-identification, access.
- [`consent-scoring-panel.md`](consent-scoring-panel.md) — recruitment + consent template for the blind
  scoring panel (and later, real therapists).

## Why this might be lighter than it looks
Much of the study uses **synthetic** patients and **already-public, licensed** testimony, and the human
participants are **professional scorers** (not vulnerable patients). The IRB may classify parts as
exempt or not-human-subjects — but **that's the IRB's call, not ours**. Confirm before collecting any
scored data.

## Hard rules regardless of IRB status
- No real patient identifiers anywhere in the repo or transcripts.
- Only ingest sources cleared in the intake register.
- Anyone actually in crisis is out of scope and directed to human help (US: 988).
